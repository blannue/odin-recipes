Peanut Butter and Chocolate Chips on Keto Bread

Description

This is another easy little snack recipe that my girlfriend has showed me. It is also a very minimal recipe that only requires three ingredients and it is scrumptious.

Ingredients

  • Keto Bread
  • Peanut Butter
  • Chocolate Chips

Steps

  1. Grab a slice of Keto bread on put it in the toaster until golden brown.
  2. Grab the peanut butter and spread about 20g on the now-made toast.
  3. Sprinkle about 20g of chocolate chips on top and lightly pat it down.
  4. Enjoy!
