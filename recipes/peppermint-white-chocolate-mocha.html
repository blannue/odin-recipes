Peppermint White Chocolate Mocha

Ingredients

  • Heavy Cream
  • Granulated Sugar
  • White Chocolate
  • Peppermint Extract
  • Whole Milk
  • Espresso
  • Whipped Cream

Steps

  1. Heat the cream and sugar on the stove over medium heat.
  2. Once it boils, pour the newly made syrup over a bowl filled with chopped white chocolate
  3. Once you let that sit for five minutes, whisk the mixture until smooth.
  4. Add one serving of milk.
  5. Heat and froth the mixture and finish with whipped cream and crushed candy canes pieces.
