Taco Bowl Recipe

Description

This is an easy cheap recipe that is one of my guiltless pleasure. It's a meal I can easily fall back on when I don't know what else to have.

Ingredients

  • 1/4lb ground beef
  • nonstick spray
  • 28g of shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 serving of the doritos of your choice
  • 30g of fat free sour cream

Steps

  1. Get a nonstick pan ready and set the burner to medium-high.
  2. Apply nonstick spray to the pan while it's preheating.
  3. Season and cook the ground beef until it is all browned.
  4. Use strainer to strain the fat from the browned beef
  5. Grab a bowl and serve up a 1/4lb of ground beef.
  6. Sprinkle the shredded cheese on top of the beef.
  7. Put the sour cream on top.
  8. Mix the contents until uniform.
  9. Add the doritos on top of the bowl and enjoy.
